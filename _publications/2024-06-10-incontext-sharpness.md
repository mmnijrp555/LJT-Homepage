---
title: "In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation"
date: 2024-06-10
venue: "ICML 2024"
author: "Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He"
link: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.xxxxxx"
---
We propose using in-context sharpness of inner representations as alerts for hallucination mitigation in large language models. Our approach leverages the dynamics of contextual representations to detect potential hallucinations before they are generated.
