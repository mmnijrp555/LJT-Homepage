---
title: "Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations"
date: 2023-09-10
venue: "NeurIPS 2023"
author: "Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He"
link: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.xxxxxx"
---
We explore composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations for flexible knowledge transfer in large language models. This approach enables combining multiple fine-tuned models in a lightweight manner without full retraining.
