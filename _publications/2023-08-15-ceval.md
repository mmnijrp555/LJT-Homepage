---
title: "C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models"
date: 2023-08-15
venue: "NeurIPS 2023"
author: "Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He"

We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level, multi-discipline evaluation suite for testing foundation models in Chinese. C-Eval covers a wide range of subjects and difficulty levels, providing a thorough assessment of model capabilities in the Chinese language.
